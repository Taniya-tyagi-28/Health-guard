RESTful Clinical API Endpoints

Fully compliant REST endpoints ready for EHR integration and automated Pytest execution.

POST /api/predict

Predicts adherence risk tier (Low, Medium, High) with confidence probabilities.

GET /api/patients

Retrieves all patients with real-time risk scores and adherence percentages.

POST /api/patients

Registers new patient and calculates initial ML risk assessment baseline.

POST /api/prescriptions

Creates new medication prescription and recalculates patient risk assessment.

POST /api/adherence

Logs dose status (Taken, Missed, Late) with automatic real-time ML risk updates.

GET /api/analytics

Provides aggregate population metrics, trend graphs, and high-risk rosters.
